| D-11 | Who assigns PO/SO numbers, in which format? | User-decided 2026-10-10 (optional scope, ADR-017): the server assigns `PO-YYMM-NNNN` / `SO-YYMM-NNNN` when the draft is saved; YYMM = month of the MySQL session clock that also sets `order_date` (UTC in this deployment); NNNN = per-prefix-per-month counter from 0001, at least four digits and growing past 9999. | D-12 | Should an approved sales order hold its stock, and what counts as available? | User-decided 2026-10-10 (optional scope, ADR-018): Admin approval reserves every line at the source warehouse in one transaction (`product_stocks.reserved_quantity`, CHECK reserved ≤ quantity) and is refused with 422 when any line exceeds available = quantity − reserved; issue consumes the reservation; cancelling an Approved order releases it; adjustments, transfers out and supplier returns cannot go below the reservation. Migration reserves for existing Approved orders and stops with a report instead of clamping. | Prevents two approved orders promising the same units; the shortage now surfaces at approval instead of at issue. Trainer may prefer issue-time checking only (the brief requires only that insufficient issue is rejected). | User decision (not trainer) |
| D-13 | May a PO/SO line use a price other than the product master? | User-decided 2026-10-10 (optional scope, ADR-019; partially supersedes D-09/ADR-015): the master price is the default and is always snapshotted by the server as `list_price`; a different price needs a trimmed reason of 3–255 characters (`price_reason`), validated in the services. Cap is global (not per customer/product); cost floor uses the current purchase price, not average cost; Admin overrides need no second approval. | User decision (not trainer) |
| D-14 | May a sales order be shipped in several parts, and how is that shown? | User-decided 2026-10-11 (optional scope, ADR-020): **adds the SO status `PartiallyFulfilled`, which is outside the official brief's SO status set** (user decision, not trainer). Lines record `issued_quantity`; Warehouse/Admin issue per-line quantities 0..remaining in one transaction per shipment; Fulfilled when every line is complete, otherwise PartiallyFulfilled. The unshipped remainder stays reserved (D-12). Admin may close the remainder of a PartiallyFulfilled order with a reason (`sales_order_closures`), releasing its reservation; the status stays PartiallyFulfilled and further issues are refused. Closing an Approved order with nothing shipped is refused (cancel instead); cancelling a PartiallyFulfilled order is refused. Outstanding SO quantity = Σ(ordered − issued) excluding closed. Idempotency hash covers the per-line quantities. | Records real distributor practice (stock arrives in parts, backorders) and keeps the shortage visible. Cost: one status beyond the brief; reports and the trainer's status list differ. Alternative inside the brief: keep the order Approved until the last shipment (status would hide partial shipments). Trainer may also prefer closing as Fulfilled or a separate Closed status. | User decision (not trainer) |
| D-15 | Should a warehouse transfer be applied in one step, or travel with goods in transit? | User-decided 2026-10-11 (optional scope, ADR-021): transfers become two-step. **Adds the operation-document statuses `Dispatched` and `Received` for Transfer documents only** (PendingApproval → Approved → Dispatched → Received; other kinds keep → Posted). They are stock-operation statuses from the user-approved ADR-009 scope, not PO/SO statuses; the brief's PO/SO status sets are unchanged. Dispatch (Admin/WarehouseStaff, after independent approval) lowers the source by the full approved quantity, never below reservations (available = quantity − reserved, checked after the row lock), and writes negative Adjustment ledger rows (reference TRANSFER). Receipt is single and complete: per line good + damaged ≤ dispatched, the rest is short; only good units enter the destination (positive Adjustment rows); damaged and short units are written off on the document with a mandatory reason, without a ledger row. Cancel only before dispatch; a lost shipment is received with 0 good units. Posting a transfer means dispatching it; repeats are no-ops. Existing Posted transfers become Received (dispatched = received = quantity), Approved ones stay Approved. Replenishment subtracts goods in transit to the warehouse. | Real distributors move stock between warehouses by truck; one-step transfers hid goods on the road and could not record damage or loss. Ledger stays Receipt/Issue/Adjustment (brief set), so stock = ledger and reports keep working. Alternatives: new ledger movement types `TransferOut/TransferIn` (rejected: changes the brief's ledger set), a quarantine warehouse for damaged goods (deferred), partial receipts (deferred). Trainer may prefer the one-step transfer of ADR-009. | User decision (not trainer) |
| D-16 | May a product be bought and sold in several units (karton, box, pcs), and how is stock kept consistent? | User-decided 2026-10-11 (Series 2, feature #1; ADR-022): **base unit = smallest unit**; every stored quantity (stock, reservations, ledger, order and operation lines, transit, returns, reorder points) is an integer in the base unit, converted only at input and display. Units per product in `product_units` (exactly one base unit with factor 1 = `products.unit`; larger units with factor ≥ 2; optional unique barcode; own master prices per unit, else base price × factor; at most one purchase and one sales default). PO/SO/operation lines store the unit snapshot (`unit_code`, `unit_factor`, `unit_quantity`); line prices are per entered unit and the discount cap / cost floor compare per unit (cost = base purchase × factor); receipts and shipments are whole line units. Admin manages units (403 otherwise); code and factor are immutable once used on a document. Replenishment rounds up to whole purchase-default units. The availability API adds `base_unit` and `units`. Migration: products named "… (N pcs/pak)" move to base pcs/pak with every quantity × N and line snapshots (old unit, N, old quantity), line totals unchanged; others keep their unit as base. | Lets a distributor buy cartons and sell cartons, boxes or pieces without fractional stock. Cost: API and report numbers for converted products are now pieces (factor × the old value); audit metadata written before the migration keeps the old unit; derived base prices are rounded to cents (stock value differs by at most Σ base quantity × Rp 0,005). No fractional units, no per-customer unit prices, no unit conversion of historical audit rows. | User decision (not trainer) |
| D-17 | Should stock be tracked per batch with expiry, shipped earliest-expiry first, and how is expired stock handled? | User-decided 2026-10-11 (Series 2, feature #2; ADR-023): **every unit belongs to a lot** (`stock_lots`: product + warehouse + lot code + expiry + unit cost per base unit; `product_stocks` stays the aggregate and the lock root, Σ lots = stock, lot = opening + Σ its ledger rows; every new ledger row names its lot). `products.tracks_expiry` makes an expiry date mandatory on new lots; food and beverage categories were flagged by the migration. **A lot is expired when its expiry date is before today in `APP_TIMEZONE`** (default Asia/Jakarta, not the UTC date of ADR-017); expired lots are never sold, shipped, transferred or counted as available (available = non-expired − reserved, used by approval, low stock and replenishment). PO receipts split each line into lots (code, expiry, quantity) adding up to the received quantity; unit cost = line price ÷ unit factor in whole cents. SO shipments allocate **FEFO** automatically (expiry ascending, no expiry last, then first receipt) and the ledger stores the allocation; no manual override. Transfers carry their lots and recreate them (same code, expiry, cost) at the destination; damage and shortage are written off per lot. Counts are per lot (or pair-level as before); supplier returns use the original receipt lot, customer returns the original issue lot (an expired one stays a not-sellable quarantine). Expired stock is written off through an Admin-approved Adjustment with purpose `ExpiredWriteOff`. A reservation that outgrows the non-expired stock is refused at issue (422) and listed in the Expiry report, never cancelled automatically. Migration: one `LEGACY` lot per pair with stock (no expiry, opening = quantity, current purchase price). `NEAR_EXPIRY_DAYS` (default 30). | FEFO and expiry blocking stop expired goods leaving the warehouse and make recalls traceable; lot costs prepare FIFO valuation. Cost: lot input on every receipt, several ledger rows per shipment line, migrated stock has no expiry (permissive LEGACY), adjustments count one lot per line, per-lot transfer receipt input and a FEFO override are not implemented, reservations can outgrow sellable stock (reported). Trainer may prefer FIFO without expiry or manual lot choice. | User decision (not trainer) |
| D-18 | How are barcodes used, and how are physical stock counts run and posted without losing concurrent movements? | User-decided 2026-10-11 (Series 2, feature #3; ADR-024). **Barcodes:** `GET /api/barcodes/{code}` resolves a `product_units.barcode` to product, unit, factor and active flags (Admin/Warehouse; Sales 403; 401/404/422; EAN-13 check digit validated). Keyboard-wedge scanners are the primary input, the camera (`BarcodeDetector`) only an optional enhancement. Scan to receive (PO) and scan to pick (SO) apply exact unit conversions only; the server stays authoritative; scan counts are not stored as form drafts. **Stock counts:** a count document **adds its own statuses Draft → Counting → Submitted → (RecountRequested → Counting) → Approved → Posted, Cancelled before posting** (count document statuses; PO/SO status sets unchanged); scope all/category/products per warehouse; only assigned counters (Admin or Warehouse accounts) count; counts are blind for counters and Warehouse; expected = snapshot + Σ ledger rows of the lot between the snapshot and the count watermark (ledger ids); variance = counted − expected; posting applies the approved variance as a delta to the current lot (Adjustment rows, reference STOCK_COUNT), refusing with 422 and listing every line that would go negative or below the reservation; recount only above tolerance (`COUNT_RECOUNT_TOLERANCE_PERCENT` 2 / `_UNITS` 0), once per line, by a different counter (enforced); approval by an Admin who counted nothing; found lots created at the current purchase price; two open counts of a warehouse never share a product. | Blind, independently approved counts that never overwrite concurrent shipments or receipts. Cost: a count briefly locks every pair of its scope at start; one recount per line; a second counter is needed for recounts; overlapping open counts are refused; physical moves between counting a shelf and saving are outside what software can see. Trainer may prefer the simpler ADR-009 count adjustment only, or "freeze the warehouse" counts. | User decision (not trainer) |
| D-19 | Should customers get their own prices, can sales quote before ordering, and how are shipments documented and billed with PPN? | User-decided 2026-10-11 (Series 2, feature #6; ADR-025). **Price lists:** one list per customer (`customers.price_list_id`), active flag and validity dates, tiers per product and unit (`min_quantity` in that unit); a new SO/quotation line takes the highest tier with `min_quantity ≤` its unit quantity, else the master unit price; the line stores `price_source` (MASTER/PRICE_LIST + list id) and `list_price` = the resolved price, so **ADR-019's reason requirement, Sales cap and "differs" now refer to the resolved price, not the master**; the cost floor applies to tiers when maintained and when used (a tier that fell below cost is refused, not silently replaced). Admin maintains lists (audited). **Quotations** `QT-YYMM-NNNN` (quotation statuses Draft → Sent → Accepted/Rejected, Cancelled; Expired computed at read time from `valid_until`, no job; not SO statuses); Sales own, Admin all; an Accepted quotation still valid converts once into an SO draft at the quoted prices (re-validated; owner = quotation creator), in one transaction with a quotation row lock. **Delivery notes** `DO-YYMM-NNNN` created in the same transaction as every shipment (lines in line unit + base, lots used, ship-to snapshot, optional vehicle/driver); historical shipments get notes from the ledger at migration (current customer address). **Invoices** `INV-YYMM-NNNN`: one live invoice per delivery note, lines = shipped quantity × SO price, PPN per the assumptions below with a settings snapshot, customer NPWP/NIK/tax name/address snapshot; payments (no overpayment, invoice row lock), computed status Unpaid/PartiallyPaid/Paid (+ Overdue), Admin void with reason only without payment (no stock effect); no invoices for history. **Tax assumptions (not facts, to be verified by a tax adviser):** prices exclude PPN; `VAT_RATE_PERCENT` 12 on DPP `VAT_DPP_NUMERATOR/VAT_DPP_DENOMINATOR` 11/12 (effective 11 %); DPP and PPN rounded half up to whole Rupiah per invoice; `INVOICE_PAYMENT_TERMS_DAYS` 30. Reports: AR aging (0–30/31–60/61–90/>90 days since invoice date, overdue) and sales by customer, CSV; Admin dashboard card. Roles enforced in services: Admin price lists/invoices/payments; Sales own quotations and read-only own delivery notes/invoices; Warehouse creates delivery notes via issue and prints them. | Removes manual retyping of customer prices and quotes, documents every shipment and bills exactly what was shipped. Cost: one more sequence lock per shipment; ADR-019 wording changes meaning; one list per customer, tiers per unit only; no credit notes, returns are not netted against invoices, no e-Faktur; the tax rule is a configurable assumption; historical delivery notes carry today's customer address. The trainer may prefer quotations/invoices out of scope. | User decision (not trainer) |
| D-20 | How is inventory valued, and where do cost of goods sold and gross margin come from? | User-decided 2026-10-11 (Series 2, feature #7; ADR-026): **FIFO per batch** — every lot keeps its fixed unit cost per base unit (ADR-023) and every outbound quantity takes the cost of the lot it physically left; the lots are chosen by the existing FEFO allocation (for products without expiry this is receipt order = FIFO), i.e. specific identification at lot level. Every ledger row with a lot stores `unit_cost` and `cost_amount` = signed quantity × lot cost (whole cents, half away from zero; DB CHECK). Transfers carry the source lot cost; damaged/short units are a transit loss at that cost. **Valuation start = the ADR-023 watermark**: earlier rows have no cost and are reported as "before valuation start", never given one; the LEGACY lots' opening value is the starting value. Invariants: lot value roll-forward to the cent, Σ lot values per pair, transit value, COGS per delivery note = its lots × lot cost. Admin-only read models: dashboard Inventory Value = lots at cost on hand + in transit (the former master-price figure stays as a labelled reference), valuation (current/as-of date, by warehouse/category/product/lot, CSV), period reconciliation per warehouse with an in-transit line (to the cent, CSV), gross margin per invoice line/customer/product (net revenue excl. PPN − COGS; void invoices excluded; shipped-not-invoiced listed apart; CSV), losses (expired write-offs, transit damage/shortage, count shortfalls; CSV), COGS/margin panels on SO, delivery note and invoice detail. Sales and Warehouse 403. | Makes inventory value and HPP reflect what the stock actually cost when supplier prices change, values goods in transit and losses, and proves every figure against the ledger. Cost: two ledger columns and a CHECK; history before the lot migration stays unvalued on existing databases; FEFO (not receipt date) decides which layer is consumed for perishables, by design; no landed costs, no write-down to net realisable value, no credit notes. The trainer may consider valuation out of scope. | User decision (not trainer) |

## Resolved engineering decisions (not trainer policy)

- Local assessment runtime stays on port 8080 with native PHP built-in server; the independent production topology uses Nginx/PHP-FPM/TLS (ADR-007).
- Transaction ownership is implemented with a dedicated shared TransactionManagerInterface, MySqlTransactionManager and explicit constructor injection (ADR-004/006). The former note that PDO ownership awaited trainer confirmation is obsolete.
- `composer test`, `test:unit`, `test:integration`, `analyse` remain the development commands; isolated Docker quality tooling is authoritative for current verification.
