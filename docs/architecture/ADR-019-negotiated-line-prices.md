# ADR-019: negotiated PO/SO line prices with a mandatory reason

Date: 10 October 2026. Decision: project owner (user), D-13. Not a trainer decision and not an official brief requirement. Requirement support: PO-01, SO-01, VAL-01, DB-01, ARCH-01, ARCH-02, SEC, TEST-01/02. **Partially supersedes ADR-015 (D-09):** the master price stays the default and the line still stores a price snapshot, but a line may now deviate from the master when a reason is given. ADR-015's Rupiah display decision is unchanged. Order statuses are unchanged (no new status).

**Context.** ADR-015 made every PO/SO line use the product master price and ignored any submitted price. That prevented typing errors and tampering but could not record a discount agreed with a customer or a price negotiated with a supplier without first editing the master, which would change every later order. The user relaxed D-09: master by default, deviations allowed with a written reason that the approver can see.

**Decision.**
- **Schema.** `purchase_order_items` and `sales_order_items` gain `list_price DECIMAL(14,2) NOT NULL` (the master price snapshot at draft creation, same type as the line price) and `price_reason VARCHAR(255) NULL`. The existing `purchase_price` / `selling_price` stays the **effective** line price, so reports, dashboard values and CSV exports read the same column as before. CHECKs: `list_price >= 0` and `(price = list_price AND price_reason IS NULL) OR (price <> list_price AND price_reason IS NOT NULL AND CHAR_LENGTH(TRIM(price_reason)) >= 3)`. The explicit `IS NOT NULL` matters: MySQL accepts a CHECK whose result is UNKNOWN, so `CHAR_LENGTH(TRIM(NULL)) >= 3` alone would let an override without a reason through.
- **Rules (server-side, `App\Service\OrderLinePricing`, used by `PurchaseOrderService` / `SalesOrderService::createDraft()`).** Amounts are compared in whole cents.
  - Empty or equal to the **current** master price → master price, reason stored as NULL (a reason sent with an unchanged price is dropped).
  - Different price → trimmed reason of 3–255 characters, otherwise 422.
  - Format: non-negative, at most 2 decimals, at most 12 integer digits (fits DECIMAL(14,2)); anything else (negative, `1e3`, `Rp 1.400,00`, arrays) → 422.
  - **SO:** never below the product's current purchase price (no below-cost sale, also for Admin) → 422. **Sales** may discount at most `SALES_MAX_DISCOUNT_PERCENT` below master (env/Config, default 10, whole number 0–100, invalid value fails at boot); **Admin** has no discount cap. Above master is allowed for both, with a reason.
  - **PO:** a different price must be greater than zero and needs a reason; Admin and Warehouse Staff create POs as before.
  - **Never trust the client's list price.** The service reads only `price` and `price_reason` per line and snapshots the master itself; `OrderItemsInput` does not even parse a posted `list_price`, and legacy item keys (`purchase_price`/`selling_price` in service arrays) are ignored.
  - Validation happens before the draft transaction, so a refused price never claims an order number (ADR-017) and writes nothing.
- **Form (shared `views/partials/order-item.php` + `order-items.js`, PO and SO).** The price is a plain `type="number" min="0" step="0.01"` input named `purchase_price` / `selling_price` (`items[n][…]` on supplementary lines), prefilled with the master price; the hint below shows "Master price: Rp …" and, when the price differs, the difference in % and Rupiah per unit. "Use master price" resets the line. The reason field is shown and `required` (client hint only) when the price differs. Changing the product resets the line to the new product's master price. `data-price` on each option still carries the master value. A 422 re-render keeps the typed price and reason. Replenishment prefill still uses the master price. The SO form states the Sales cap; the PO form states the PO rule.
- **Form drafts (ADR-014).** Price and reason are ordinary input and are saved/restored; `list_price` is in the excluded-name pattern and never stored (it is not a form field anyway).
- **Approval visibility.** PO and SO detail pages add "Master Price", "Difference vs Master" (`-5,00% · -Rp 50,00 per unit · -Rp 500,00 line`, via `Money::rupiah`) and "Price Reason" columns when the order has at least one overridden line, and mark the line "Negotiated". A pending SO with overrides shows "N lines priced away from master; total difference Rp …" above the Approve button; a Draft PO shows the same notice above Mark Ordered, because Admin orders it. Sales still sees only own orders (unchanged 403).
- **Timeline / audit.** PO/SO creation is audited only by the request-level `RequestAuditRecorder` row, which has no document id and request metadata only. Minimal approach instead of changing the audit pattern: the timeline "Document created" event shows "N lines priced away from master (reasons on the order lines)", derived from the stored line data.
- **Readiness probe** resolves `list_price, price_reason` on both item tables, so run the migration before activating the new image.

**Migration.** `database/migrations/20261010-negotiated-prices.sql`, run by `scripts/migrate-negotiated-prices.php` (schema version `negotiated-price-1`). Additive and idempotent: add `list_price` nullable, backfill `list_price = line price` (those lines used the master under ADR-015; `updated_at` is kept), make it NOT NULL only while still nullable (also completes an interrupted run), add `price_reason`, then the CHECKs if missing. The runner verifies the columns and reports lines, overridden lines and overrides without reason (must be 0). Stock, ledger, statuses and line prices are never touched.

**Seed.** The generator adds a small deterministic share of overrides chosen from document counters (no extra `mt_rand` call, so every other seed table is byte-identical to the previous seed): 15 SO lines discounted by Sales (4 %, 6 % or 8 %, within the 10 % cap) with reasons such as "Promo akhir bulan" and "Volume order > 50 karton", one SO line 5 % above master ("Biaya pengiriman ekspres ke luar kota disepakati pelanggan"), and 12 PO lines 2–3 % below master with supplier negotiation reasons. The summary checks `price_override_without_reason = 0`, `so_lines_below_purchase_price = 0` (purchase prices do not change during the seed, so the current master is the price at that time) and `sales_discount_above_cap = 0`, besides the existing ledger/reservation/sequence invariants.

**Consequences.** Discounts and negotiated supplier prices can be recorded per order with an auditable reason, and the approver sees the deviation before approving or ordering. Typing errors are possible again but are bounded (reason required, below-cost and Sales-cap rules, highlighted on approval). The master price read happens at validation, before the draft transaction; if an Admin edits the master in that instant, the snapshot is the value that was validated, which is consistent with the stored line. The cap is global, not per product, customer or user. Admin overrides are not separately approved (Admin approves the SO anyway). The below-cost rule compares with the current purchase price, not a weighted average cost. A master selling price that is itself below the purchase price is still accepted unchanged (master data quality is a separate concern).

**Tests.** `NegotiatedPriceTest` (equal/empty price → master with null reason, missing/short/long reason 422, malformed prices 422, column maximum, below cost 422 for Admin, Sales cap vs Admin, configurable cap and invalid configuration, above master with reason, client `list_price` ignored, PO override by Warehouse/Admin and zero refused, refused price claims no order number, input parser, summary/labels). `NegotiatedPriceControllerTest` (editable prefilled price, reset, reason, cap hint, no `readonly`/`list_price`, 422 keeps typed price and required reason, SO detail columns and approval notice above Approve, Sales owner view, other Sales 403, master-priced order unchanged, PO detail and Draft notice, PO without reason 422). `NegotiatedPriceIntegrationTest` (MySQL: seed invariants, persistence through findById/all/search and after receipt, rules before writes, DB CHECKs, timeline detail, migration backfill from an older schema and an interrupted state, idempotent re-run, `updated_at` kept). JavaScript: `order-items.test.js` (cents, difference, hint), `form-drafts.test.js` (price/reason kept, `list_price` excluded). Updated ADR-015 tests are listed in `docs/testing/negotiated-price-2026-10-10.md`.

Note 12 October 2026 (D-16, ADR-022, user decision): line prices, `list_price`, the Sales discount cap and the below-cost rule are now per the line's entered unit. The master price of a unit is its own unit price, else base price × factor; cost per unit = base purchase price × factor. Line total = unit_quantity × price.

Note 12 October 2026 (D-19, ADR-025, user decision): for SO lines the server's `list_price` snapshot is now the **resolved** price — the customer's price list tier for the product, unit and quantity, else the master unit price — and the reason requirement, the Sales discount cap and the price-difference display are measured against it (`price_source` records MASTER or PRICE_LIST). The cost floor also applies to a tier itself. PO lines are unchanged.
