# ADR-021: warehouse transfers with goods in transit

Date: 12 October 2026. Decision: project owner (user), D-15, approved with Series 2 on 2026-10-11 (feature #5). **Not a trainer decision and not an official brief requirement.** Transfers are optional scope from ADR-009. This ADR adds two *operation-document* statuses, `Dispatched` and `Received`, for Transfer documents only. The brief's PO and SO status sets are unchanged, and so is the ledger movement set (Receipt, Issue, Adjustment). Requirement support: WH-01, PRD-01, DASH-01, REPORT-01, DB-01, ARCH-01, ARCH-02, TEST-01/02. Builds on ADR-002 (row locks), ADR-003 (ledger), ADR-004 (document lock first), ADR-009 (stock operations, signed Adjustment ledger), ADR-010 (work queue), ADR-012 (outstanding), ADR-016/018 (low stock on available, reservation) and ADR-020 (partial SO fulfilment, unchanged).

**Context.** A Transfer was posted in one transaction: source −qty and destination +qty as paired signed Adjustment rows. Distributors move stock between warehouses by truck over days. Goods on the road were invisible, the destination looked stocked before anything arrived, and damage or loss on arrival could not be recorded.

**Decision.**
- **Lifecycle (Transfer only).** PendingApproval → Approved (independent Admin approval, unchanged) → **Dispatched** → **Received**. Rejected and Cancelled are unchanged. Cancel is allowed only before dispatch (PendingApproval/Approved). A dispatched transfer must be received, with 0 good units if the shipment was lost. `Posted` is no longer used for transfers; Adjustment and the returns still end Posted. The database guards this with `chk_operation_transit_status`.
- **Schema.** `inventory_operations`: status ENUM gains `Dispatched`, `Received`; new columns `dispatched_by/at`, `received_by/at` (FKs to users) and `receipt_reason VARCHAR(500)`; `chk_operation_transit_stamps` ties the stamps to the status. `inventory_operation_items`: `dispatched_quantity`, `received_quantity`, `damaged_quantity`, `short_quantity` (all nullable INT UNSIGNED), with `chk_operation_items_dispatched` (dispatched = quantity: dispatch is always the whole approved line) and `chk_operation_items_receipt` (received + damaged + short = dispatched once received). In transit per line = dispatched − received − damaged − short; written off = damaged + short.
- **Ledger representation (chosen).** Signed `Adjustment` rows with `reference_type = 'TRANSFER'`, exactly as before but split in time: dispatch appends one **negative** row per line at the source, receipt appends one **positive** row per line at the destination for the **good** units only. The sign and warehouse identify transfer out/in, so no new column or movement type is needed. Rejected alternative: new `TransferOut`/`TransferIn` movement types. They would change the brief's ledger set (SDD 23.2) and every report and reconciliation query. A `transfer_stage` column would only repeat what the sign already says. Consequence: `quantity = Σ ledger` keeps holding per pair at every moment. In-transit units belong to no warehouse and are tracked on the document lines.
- **Dispatch** (`BusinessOperationService::dispatch`, route `POST /inventory-operations/dispatch`). Admin/WarehouseStaff only (403 before any lock). There is no per-user warehouse assignment in the system, so "source staff" is a role check plus the work-queue label, not a warehouse scope. One transaction: lock the transfer (`FOR UPDATE OF o`). A Dispatched or Received transfer returns as a no-op (ADR-009 style). Otherwise independent approval is required (422), and source and destination pairs must be active. `StockService::dispatchTransfer()` then locks the source rows in product order and checks **available = quantity − reserved ≥ qty after the lock** for every line (422 listing the short lines, nothing changes, reservations are never consumed). It lowers `quantity`, appends the negative rows, marks the document Dispatched with `dispatched_quantity = quantity`, and writes the strict audit row `inventory-operations.dispatch` (entity TRANSFER) with `dispatched_units`, `dispatch[]` and `destination_id`.
- **Posting a transfer** (`POST /inventory-operations/post` or `post()`) now dispatches it. Existing scripts and forms therefore keep working, and repeats stay no-ops.
- **Receipt** (`BusinessOperationService::receive`, route `POST /inventory-operations/receive` with `received[product_id]`, `damaged[product_id]` and `receipt_reason`). Admin/WarehouseStaff only. The payload is validated before the transaction: whole numbers ≥ 0 (422). One transaction: lock the transfer. Received → no-op. Not Dispatched → 422. Every line must be given (or no lines at all, meaning every unit arrived in good condition, for trusted callers). good + damaged ≤ dispatched (422), short = the rest, and foreign lines are refused (422). **Any damaged or short unit needs a reason** (1–500 characters, 422 otherwise). `StockService::receiveTransfer()` locks the destination rows in product order, checks overflow, raises `quantity` by the good units, appends the positive rows, marks the document Received with the per-line figures and the reason, and writes the strict audit row `inventory-operations.receive` with `received_units`, `damaged_units`, `short_units`, `written_off_units`, `reason` and `receipt[]`. If nothing arrived in good condition, the receipt has no ledger row but still commits state and audit.
- **Why a single, complete receipt.** The truck arrives once. Partial receipts would need a remaining-in-transit state per line, repeated discrepancy handling and an "in transit forever" closure, with no user requirement behind them. A late second truck is a new transfer.
- **Write-off.** Damaged and short units leave the system at receipt. They never enter sellable stock and have no ledger row (the ledger records warehouse stock), so stock = ledger is untouched. The document lines, the receipt reason and the audit row are the loss reference. A damaged-goods quarantine warehouse remains out of scope.
- **Lock order.** Document first, then stock rows (ADR-004). Dispatch locks one warehouse in product order and receipt does the same, which is a subsequence of the global (product, warehouse) order used by `adjust()`. So a dispatch racing an SO approval (SO lock → source rows) cannot deadlock, and whichever locks the row second re-checks available. Two receipts of the same transfer serialize on the document lock; the second sees Received and returns.
- **Read models.**
  - Transfer detail: From → To with warehouse names, an in-transit badge (`status-in-transit`), dates and people (approved, dispatched by, received by), and per line Quantity / Dispatched / In transit / Received (good) / Damaged / Short. It also shows written-off units, "Received with discrepancy" and the reason. The Dispatch form appears when Approved; the receive form (good + damaged per line, reason) appears when Dispatched. Cancel is hidden after dispatch, and "Post Stock Changes" is never shown for transfers.
  - New page **In Transit** (`GET /inventory-operations/in-transit`, sidebar entry, Admin/WarehouseStaff): open Dispatched transfers, oldest dispatch first, with lines, units, dispatcher and days in transit.
  - Product detail: "In Transit (inbound)" per destination warehouse and in the summary. It is informational only and never added to Quantity or Available (`ProductStock::inTransitInbound()`).
  - Work queue (updates ADR-010): `TransferDispatch` "Dispatch approved transfer" and `TransferReceive` "Receive transfer". The label names source → destination. "Stock posting" no longer lists transfers.
  - Outstanding report (updates ADR-012): Dispatched transfers are open OP documents in the Admin and fulfilment scopes, with OutstandingQty = units in transit; DaysSinceApproval also applies.
  - Timeline: "Transfer dispatched (in transit)" (N units left the source) and "Transfer received" ("Received G good units; written off D damaged and S short" plus the reason). Ledger rows read "Adjustment (transfer out)" / "Adjustment (transfer in)".
  - Stock Movements report and CSV: unchanged columns. Transfer rows stay Movement `Adjustment`, ReferenceType `TRANSFER`, with signed quantities. Net movement over a period therefore falls by the units in transit and by write-offs, which is the correct physical figure for warehouse stock.
- **Low stock and replenishment.** Low stock stays on available (ADR-016/018); goods in transit do not hide a low destination. Replenishment now also subtracts goods in transit to the warehouse, just like open PO supply: `suggested = max(0, effective − available − open PO supply − in transit inbound)`. The page shows "+ N in transit" next to the PO supply.
- **New invariants** (`TransferTransitChecks`, used by the migration runner, seed summary and tests): the negative TRANSFER rows at the source per line sum to `dispatched_quantity`; the positive rows at the destination sum to `received_quantity`; Dispatched ⇒ every line dispatched and none received, Received ⇒ every line received, other statuses ⇒ nothing dispatched; **Σ dispatched − Σ received − Σ damaged − Σ short = Σ dispatched of open Dispatched transfers**; a Received transfer with a write-off has a reason.
- **Readiness probe** selects the new columns, so the migration must run before the new image is activated.

**Migration.** `database/migrations/20261012-transit-transfer.sql` (idempotent: ENUM extension, columns and FKs, mapping, CHECKs) is run by `scripts/migrate-transit-transfer.php`. Existing **Posted** transfers were applied atomically, so they become **Received** with dispatched = received = quantity, damaged = short = 0, and dispatched/received by/at copied from posted_by/at. **Approved** transfers stay Approved and follow the new flow. Stock and ledger are never touched or re-applied. The runner verifies the columns, the transit checks and stock = ledger. If any count is non-zero it prints the counts, exits 2 and does not record schema version `transit-transfer-1`. Re-running after success is a no-op.

**Seed.** One rebalancing transfer about every four days (40 in total). Products plentiful at the source (available > 3 × reorder point) and low at the destination (< 2 × reorder point) are moved by source warehouse staff, approved by an Admin, dispatched 1–2 days later and received 1–3 days after that by destination staff. Damage is applied when `t % 5 = 2` and a 1-unit shortage when `t % 7 = 4`, each with an Indonesian reason. The receipts of the last three are planned after the window: two remain **in transit**, and the last is approved but not yet dispatched. Rebuilt once: 40 transfers (Received 37, Dispatched 2, Approved 1), 39 dispatched, 0 dispatches refused, 7 receipts with damage, 5 with shortage, 23 units written off, 109 units in transit. Summary: `ledger_mismatches 0`, `negative_stock 0`, `reservation_mismatches 0`, `transfer_dispatch_ledger_mismatches 0`, `transfer_receipt_ledger_mismatches 0`, `transfer_status_mismatches 0`, `in_transit_equation_mismatch 0`, `transfer_discrepancy_without_reason 0`, plus every earlier check at 0. `inventory_operations` and `inventory_operation_items` are now part of the generated seed. The document history differs from the previous seed because transfers move stock.

**Consequences.** Goods on the road are visible and never counted twice. Damage and loss on arrival are recorded with a reason and never become sellable. Stock = ledger holds at every moment. Two statuses outside the earlier ADR-009 set are added (documented, D-15); the trainer may prefer the one-step transfer. There is no partial dispatch, no partial receipt, no quarantine stock, no per-user warehouse scoping and no in-transit valuation (FIFO valuation arrives in a later stage).

**Tests.** `TransitTransferTest` (unit) covers: dispatch to transit using available only, dispatch refused over reservations with nothing changed, post = dispatch and repeated dispatch/receive/post as no-ops, receipt all good, damaged + short with the mandatory reason, a lost shipment received with 0 good, invalid quantities (over dispatched, foreign, missing, negative, non-numeric, empty), receipt before dispatch and dispatch before approval refused, no cancel after dispatch, authorization and transfers only, the In Transit list, controller and pages, work queue, timeline text and `ProductStock` in-transit. `TransitTransferIntegrationTest` (MySQL) covers: seed invariants, dispatch/receive persistence (ledger, lines, stamps, audit), reservations after the lock, the DB CHECKs, read models (product detail, replenishment, work queue, outstanding, timeline, stock report), and the migration (conflict report, repair, repeatable run, Posted → Received, Approved stays and then follows the new flow, stock unchanged). `ParallelStockIntegrationTest` (real processes) covers: a dispatch racing an SO approval on the same source pair never violates available, two concurrent receipts of one transfer apply once, a concurrent post replay dispatches once, and opposite transfers conserve the total. HTTP: in-transit scenarios in `tests/HTTP/end-to-end.py` and `tests/HTTP/business-enhancements.py`. Evidence: `docs/testing/transit-transfer-2026-10-12.md`.

Note 12 October 2026 (D-17, ADR-023, user decision): dispatch picks FEFO non-expired lots and records them in `inventory_operation_lots`; receipt recreates them at the destination (same code, expiry, unit cost) and writes damaged/short units off per lot (good → damaged → short over the lots in dispatch order). The TRANSFER Adjustment rows are now one per lot.

Note 12 October 2026 (D-20, ADR-026, user decision): goods in transit are now valued at the cost of the lot they left (out rows at the source lot cost, in rows at the same cost); damaged and short units are a transit loss valued at that cost on the losses report and in the period reconciliation's in-transit line. This closes the "no in-transit valuation" consequence above.
