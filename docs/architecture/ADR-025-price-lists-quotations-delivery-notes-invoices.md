# ADR-025: customer price lists, quotations, delivery notes and invoices with PPN

Date: 12 October 2026. Decision: project owner (user), D-19, approved with Series 2 on 2026-10-11 (feature #6). **Not a trainer decision and not an official brief requirement.** PO and SO status sets are unchanged and no ledger movement type is added. Three new document kinds get their own lifecycles (quotation statuses; delivery notes have none; invoice payment state is computed, not stored). Requirement support: SO-01, CUS/master data (PRD-01 parties), DASH-01, REPORT-01, VAL-01, DB-01, SEC, ARCH-01, ARCH-02, TEST-01/02. Builds on ADR-004 (document lock first), ADR-008 (replay), ADR-015/019 (line prices), ADR-017 (sequences), ADR-020 (shipments), ADR-022 (units) and ADR-023 (lots). Their invariants are unchanged.

## Context

A distributor sells to wholesale, modern and traditional outlets at different prices, often quotes before the order, hands a *surat jalan* to the driver with every shipment and bills what was shipped, with PPN. Until now an SO line started at the product master price (ADR-015/019), there was no quotation, shipments existed only as ledger rows grouped for display (`ShipmentHistory`) and there was no invoice.

## Decision

### F1 — price lists and tiered prices

- **Schema.** `price_lists (name UNIQUE, description, is_active, valid_from, valid_to NULL = open, CHECK valid_to ≥ valid_from)`, `price_list_items (price_list_id, product_id, unit_code, min_quantity ≥ 1 in that unit, unit_price ≥ 0, UNIQUE(list, product, unit, min_quantity))`, `customers.price_list_id` (nullable FK, one list per customer). A unit code used by a price list item counts as "used" for ADR-022 factor immutability.
- **Resolution** (`App\Service\PriceResolver`, one rule for the SO form, quotations and the server): for customer C, product P, line unit U and line unit quantity Q at today's business date (`APP_TIMEZONE`, from the session clock as in ADR-023, so the seed's `SET TIMESTAMP` gives historical dates): take C's price list when it is active and `valid_from ≤ today ≤ valid_to`; among its items for (P, U) take the one with the **highest `min_quantity ≤ Q`**. Otherwise the master unit price (ADR-022: the unit's own price, else base × factor). Units are matched exactly (a karton tier does not price a pcs line).
- **What the line stores.** `sales_order_items.price_source` (`MASTER` | `PRICE_LIST`) and `price_list_id` (CHECK: set exactly for `PRICE_LIST`); `list_price` = **the resolved price**. **ADR-019 semantics shift from "master price" to "resolved price":** the reason requirement, the Sales discount cap and the "differs" display are measured against the resolved price. The cost floor is unchanged (base purchase price × unit factor). A tier price that is below the current cost when a line is priced (the purchase price rose after the list was maintained) is refused with 422 rather than silently falling back to master, so an Admin updates the list.
- **Maintenance.** Admin only (403 in `PriceListService` before input parsing): create/edit/deactivate lists, add/edit/delete items, assign/unassign customers. An item below the current cost per unit is refused (cost floor); the unit must be an active unit of an active product. Every change appends a strict audit row (`price-lists.create|update|item-add|item-update|item-delete|assign`). Lists are deactivated rather than deleted (SO lines reference them).
- **Form.** The SO and quotation forms call `GET /api/price-resolution?customer_id&product_id&unit_code&quantity` (Admin/Sales; Warehouse 403; 401 anonymous; 422 invalid) from `public/assets/js/price-resolution.js` whenever customer, product, unit or quantity changes (debounced) and show the resolved price, its source ("Price list Grosir, from 10 karton") and the next tier. A price the user did not override follows the resolved price; an overridden price is kept and its difference is recomputed. Every change takes a new ticket at once and shows "Checking list price…", so an answer to an earlier request (or for another product or unit) is never applied. **The server re-resolves on save and never trusts the client** (no list price or source is read from the request).

### F2 — quotations

- **Schema.** `quotations (quotation_number UNIQUE, customer, source warehouse, status, quote_date, valid_until ≥ quote_date, notes, created_by, sent_at, decided_by/decided_at, rejection_reason, cancelled_by/cancelled_at)` and `quotation_items` with the same columns and CHECKs as SO lines (unit snapshot, `unit_price`, `list_price`, `price_reason`, `price_source`, `price_list_id`). Numbers `QT-YYMM-NNNN` from the ADR-017 sequence (prefix CHECK widened to PO, SO, QT, DO, INV).
- **Statuses** (quotation statuses, not SO statuses): `Draft → Sent → Accepted | Rejected`, `Cancelled` from Draft, Sent or an unconverted Accepted quotation. **Expired is computed at read time**, not stored and not set by a job: a Draft or Sent quotation whose `valid_until` is before today (`APP_TIMEZONE`) is shown and filtered as `Expired` and can no longer be sent or accepted (it can still be cancelled). Chosen over a job because nothing has to run for the status to be right and no stored state can be stale. An Accepted quotation keeps its status after `valid_until`; it simply cannot be converted any more.
- **Access.** Sales creates and acts on their own quotations; Admin sees and acts on all; Warehouse 403. Enforced in `QuotationService`.
- **Pricing** is the F1 pipeline (resolution, override with reason, Sales cap, cost floor), shared with the SO through `SalesLineBuilder`.
- **Conversion** (`POST /quotations/convert`): one transaction — lock the quotation (`FOR UPDATE`), require Accepted, not converted, `today ≤ valid_until`; re-validate each quoted line against current data (active product and unit, unchanged unit factor, **cost floor on the quoted price**, Sales cap against the quoted list price); claim `SO-YYMM-NNNN`; insert the SO Draft with `sales_orders.quotation_id` (UNIQUE) and per line `quotation_item_id` (UNIQUE), the quoted price, list price, reason and source. The SO belongs to the quotation's owner (so the Sales owner sees and submits it); the audit row records the converting user. **One quotation produces at most one SO**: the quotation lock serializes two conversions (the second sees the SO and gets 422) and the UNIQUE key is the final guard. Quoted prices carry over even when the price list changed since; an expired quotation is refused (the user creates a new quotation or an SO at current prices).
- **Print page** `/quotations/print?id=`.

### F3 — delivery notes (surat jalan)

- **Schema.** `delivery_notes (delivery_number DO-YYMM-NNNN UNIQUE, sales_order_id, shipment_number (UNIQUE per SO), warehouse, customer, ship_to_name, ship_to_address, vehicle NULL, driver NULL, prepared_by, shipped_at, origin Issue|Migration)`, `delivery_note_lines (sales_order_item_id, product, unit_code, unit_factor, unit_quantity, quantity = unit_quantity × unit_factor)`, `delivery_note_lots (line, ledger_id UNIQUE → stock_ledger, lot_id, lot_code, expiry_date, quantity)`.
- **Same transaction as the issue.** `SalesOrderService::issue()` (ADR-020) passes a callback to `StockService::issue()`; after the stock rows, lots and ledger rows are written and the order lines record the shipment, the same callback calls `DeliveryNoteService::createForShipment()`: claim `DO-…` (sequence row locked after the stock locks; no other path locks stock and then the DO row in the opposite order, so no cycle), snapshot the customer's name and address, one line per shipped SO line in the line unit plus base, one lot row per Issue ledger row (FEFO allocation from ADR-023). The MySQL repository links each lot row to its ledger row inside the transaction (`ledger_id` UNIQUE), so a delivery note can never claim another shipment's rows. Any failure rolls back stock, ledger, order and note together. Vehicle (≤ 40) and driver (≤ 80) are optional and validated before the transaction. A replayed issue (ADR-008) creates nothing.
- **Access.** Warehouse and Admin see and print all; Sales sees notes of their own SOs; enforced in `DeliveryNoteService`. List `/delivery-notes` (search, filter "not invoiced"), print page `/delivery-notes/show?id=` with print CSS and `window.print()`.
- **Migration of history.** `DeliveryNoteMigration` (one transaction) groups every SO Issue ledger row that is not yet on a delivery note into shipments exactly as `ShipmentHistory` does (same time and performer, consecutive, a repeated product lot starts a new shipment), in ledger order, and creates one note per shipment with `origin = Migration`, `shipped_at` and `prepared_by` from the ledger, the number from the sequence of the shipment's month, and the **current** customer name and address (the historical address is not recorded anywhere; documented). Vehicle and driver stay empty. A shipment whose base quantity is not a whole number of line units is a conflict: the runner reports it, rolls back and exits 2. Invoices are **not** created for history: billing history lives outside this system and inventing invoices would create receivables nobody owes.

### F4 — invoices with PPN

**Tax assumptions — to be verified by a tax adviser; they are configuration, not facts:**
- prices (master, price list, SO lines) **exclude PPN**;
- `VAT_RATE_PERCENT` (default 12, whole number 0–100) is applied to a DPP of `VAT_DPP_NUMERATOR / VAT_DPP_DENOMINATOR` (default 11/12, 1 ≤ numerator ≤ denominator ≤ 1000) of the net amount, i.e. an effective 11 % with the defaults (DPP nilai lain);
- rounding: **DPP and PPN are each rounded to whole Rupiah, half up, once per invoice** (not per line): `DPP = round_half_up(subtotal × num / den)`, `PPN = round_half_up(DPP × rate / 100)`, `total = subtotal + PPN`; computed in integer cents (`App\Support\TaxSettings`);
- `INVOICE_PAYMENT_TERMS_DAYS` (default 30, 0–365) sets the due date.
All four settings come from config/.env.example and are validated at boot (`ApplicationBootstrap`); an invalid value stops the application.

- **One invoice per delivery note** (bill what was shipped). `invoices (invoice_number INV-YYMM-NNNN, delivery_note_id, sales_order_id, customer, invoice_date, due_date, customer tax snapshot, subtotal, dpp_amount, vat_amount, total_amount, vat_rate_percent, vat_dpp_numerator, vat_dpp_denominator, paid_amount, voided_*)`; `active_delivery_note_id` (generated: the note while not void) is UNIQUE, so at most one live invoice per note; a voided invoice frees the note for a corrected invoice. CHECKs recompute DPP, PPN and total from the snapshot in integer arithmetic, `0 ≤ paid_amount ≤ total`, and a void invoice has no payment.
- **Lines** = delivery note line unit quantity × the SO line's effective (negotiated/resolved) unit price; `invoice_lines.amount = unit_quantity × unit_price` (CHECK).
- **Customer tax fields** (optional master data): NPWP (15 or 16 digits; dots, dashes and spaces are stripped — loose format check, no checksum), NIK (16 digits), tax name and tax address; snapshotted on the invoice with name and address.
- **Lifecycle.** Admin creates an invoice from an un-invoiced delivery note (lock the note `FOR UPDATE`, re-check, claim the number, insert; two concurrent creations serialize on the note and the second gets 422; the UNIQUE key is the final guard). Admin records payments (date not before the invoice date and not in the future, amount > 0 with at most 2 decimals, method Transfer/Cash/Giro/Other, reference): lock the invoice `FOR UPDATE`, refuse when void or when paid + amount > total ("overpayment"), insert the payment and raise `paid_amount` in the same transaction. **Status is computed**, never stored: Void, Paid (paid = total), PartiallyPaid, Unpaid; Overdue is shown when not paid and the due date has passed. Admin voids with a reason only while no payment exists; voiding never touches stock, ledger or the delivery note.
- **Access.** Admin creates, pays, voids and sees everything; Sales sees invoices of their own SOs read-only; Warehouse 403. Enforced in `InvoiceService`.
- **Reports** (Admin): AR aging `/reports/receivables` (open invoices by customer in age buckets 0–30 / 31–60 / 61–90 / > 90 days since the invoice date, overdue amount) and sales by customer `/reports/sales-by-customer` (net = subtotal, PPN, total of non-void invoices in a date range), each with CSV. Dashboard card for Admin: outstanding receivables and overdue amount.
- **Print page** `/invoices/print?id=`.

### Invariants (`SalesDocumentChecks`, all must be 0)

`issue_rows_without_delivery_note` (every SO Issue ledger row is on exactly one note), `delivery_note_line_mismatches` (line = Σ its lots, base = unit × factor, lot rows match their ledger rows), `delivery_note_issue_mismatches` (Σ note lines per SO line = issued quantity), `invoice_line_mismatches` (lines = note quantities × SO prices), `invoice_total_mismatches` (subtotal = Σ lines; DPP, PPN, total recomputed exactly from the snapshot), `invoice_payment_mismatches` (paid = Σ payments ≤ total; void ⇒ no payment), `quotation_conversion_mismatches` (≤ 1 SO per quotation, converted only from Accepted, converted lines carry the quoted price), `price_source_mismatches` (source and list id consistent; a price-list line names an item of that list for its product and unit).

## Seed

The generator (same services as the app) sets up four lists before the document window — Grosir (karton 97/95/93 % from 1/10/50), Minimarket Modern (valid April–December; pcs 99 % from 1 and 97 % from 24, inner box 97 %), Toko Tradisional (pcs 99 % from 6, 98 % from 12 for staples) and Promo Ramadan 2026 (valid until 30 April; two supermarkets then fall back to master) — with tiers in whole Rupiah and never below cost, and assigns 63 customers by outlet type (customers 1–3 stay on master prices for the test fixtures); companies get an NPWP. About one order in fifteen starts as a quotation that is sent, accepted and converted; 34 standalone quotations cover every status. Every shipment carries a vehicle and driver; Admin invoices most delivery notes the next day; payments follow the invoice id (most paid within terms, some in two parts, a few partial or never paid), one invoice is voided and re-issued. Rebuilt once: 74 quotations (Accepted 49 of which 43 converted, Rejected 7, Cancelled 5, Draft 1, Sent 2, Expired 10 as of 12 October), 603 SO, 1,621 price-list lines, 664 shipments = 664 delivery notes, 592 invoices (Paid 498, PartiallyPaid 14, Unpaid 79, Void 1; 14 overdue), 534 payments, 73 notes not invoiced. Every new and earlier invariant 0 (`docs/testing/sales-documents-2026-10-12.md`).

## Migration result on the demo database (8080)

529 orders with shipments → 529 delivery notes created from 2,820 Issue rows, no conflicts, every check 0, no invoices; re-run is a no-op. The dry run previews the grouping and conflicts before the tables exist.

## Lock order

Issue: SO → stock pairs (product order) → lots → DO sequence row. Invoice: delivery note → INV sequence row. Payment/void: invoice row. Conversion: quotation → SO sequence row → product unit share locks. Quotation creation: QT sequence row → unit share locks. No path takes two of these in the opposite order.

## Consequences

Customers get their agreed prices automatically, quotes become orders without retyping, every shipment has a printable surat jalan and every invoice is reproducible from its snapshot. Costs: one more sequence row lock per shipment; ADR-019's "master" wording now means "resolved price"; one price list per customer (no stacking, no per-customer product exceptions, no promotions by date inside a list); tiers are per unit, not converted across units; no credit notes, returns are not netted against invoices (a customer return after invoicing needs a manual adjustment outside the system); invoice status is computed so reports read payments; the tax rule is an assumption; historical delivery notes use the current customer address; e-Faktur numbering/export is out of scope.
