## Known gaps

- No units import (CSV product import still creates the base unit from `unit`); units are added on the product page.
- Audit metadata written before the migration keeps the numbers of its time (old units), e.g. timeline texts "Reserved N units" of older approvals.
- API, reports and CSV quantities of converted products are now in pieces (factor × the old values); clients must read `base_unit`.
- Transfer receipts (good/damaged) are entered in base units; counts are entered in one unit per line (count loose stock in pieces).
- Forms opened before the migration and submitted afterwards would post base quantities; the app was stopped during the data step.
- No fractional units, per-customer unit prices or unit-level stock.
