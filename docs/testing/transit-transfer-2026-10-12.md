## Known gaps

- No partial dispatch or partial receipt (a late second truck is a new transfer), no quarantine warehouse for damaged goods, no in-transit valuation.
- There is no per-user warehouse assignment, so "source staff dispatch / destination staff receive" is a role check (Admin/WarehouseStaff) plus work-queue labelling, not a warehouse scope.
- A destination pair that is deactivated while goods are in transit can still be received, so the goods are never stuck; this is intentional.
