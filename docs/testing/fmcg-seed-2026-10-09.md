# FMCG demo seed — 9 October 2026

User request: replace the whole dataset with realistic-looking FMCG data (3 warehouses, 500 SKUs, 1,000 PO/SO documents). User choices: replace the base seed (tests included), 1,000 documents in total, history spread over six months, keep the demo accounts.

**How it is built.** `scripts/build-fmcg-seed.py` runs `scripts/generate-fmcg-seed.php` in the quality test container against the disposable `*_test` database. It then dumps the data below the `-- ==== Generated FMCG seed` marker of `database/schema-and-seed.sql`. The schema part above the marker is unchanged, and the old demo inserts were removed. The generator:
- creates master data and products through `ProductService`, which initializes zero balances;
- runs every document step through the real services: `PurchaseOrderService`, `SalesOrderService` and `OrderExceptionService`, with receipts and issues through `StockService` and the ledger;
- records request-style audit entries for create, order, submit, approve and cancel, exactly as `RequestAuditRecorder` would;
- moves the MySQL session clock with `SET TIMESTAMP`, so `CURRENT_DATE`, `NOW()` and `CURRENT_TIMESTAMP` give historical dates without editing rows afterwards.

It is seeded (`mt_srand`), so rebuilding gives the same data.

**Contents.**
- **Organisation:** fictional distributor "Sumber Niaga", invented brands, `.test` e-mail domains.
- **Master data:** 3 warehouses (Cikarang, Surabaya, Makassar); 12 categories; 500 SKUs (units karton, galon, jeriken, sak, bal; Rupiah prices; reorder points by sales velocity); 15 principals (each PO only contains its own brands); 66 outlets in three regions; 17 users (the 5 demo accounts plus 12 named staff).
- **Purchase orders:** 400 between 10 April and 8 October 2026. Status: 349 Received, 16 PartiallyReceived (9 closed remainders), 13 Ordered, 4 Draft, 18 Cancelled.
- **Sales orders:** 600. Status: 524 Fulfilled, 10 PendingApproval, 9 Approved, 3 Draft, 54 Cancelled (28 rejected with a reason).
- **Stock and history:** 6,405 ledger rows and about 3,500 audit rows. Stock equals the ledger for all 1,500 product/warehouse pairs, with no negative stock and 22 pairs below reorder point (low-stock and replenishment pages have data).
- **Size:** seed file about 1.9 MB. A full integration reset still runs in about 24 s for the whole PHP suite.

**Test changes.** Only tests that read the seeded database changed; unit tests with in-memory fixtures were not touched.
- **Renamed references:** demo identifiers were mapped to seed rows (`SKU-DEMO-001` → `BIS-0001`, `SKU-DEMO-002` → `MIN-0038`, Main/Secondary Warehouse → Gudang Pusat Cikarang / Gudang Distribusi Surabaya, Demo Supplier/Customer One → PT Tirta Alam Sejahtera / Minimarket Lancar Jaya Bekasi), in integration tests, `tests/HTTP/session-smoke.py`, `tests/HTTP/load.py`, `scripts/verify-operations.py` and `tests/Browser/demo-screenshots.cjs`.
- **Closure tests:** now pick a PartiallyReceived PO whose remainder is not already closed.
- **Business ledger test:** counts ledger rows for its own products instead of the whole table.
- **Master-data tests:** expect three warehouses.
- **Negative-stock test:** it previously passed only because of an unknown column (`w.code`). It now updates a real balance to -1 and is rejected by the `CHECK` constraint.
